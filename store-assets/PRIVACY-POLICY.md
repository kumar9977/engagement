# Privacy Policy — Engagement Assistant

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Engagement Assistant is a browser extension that helps you write a comment
faster on LinkedIn and Instagram. You review every suggestion and post it
yourself. This policy explains, in plain words, what we handle and why.

## What we handle

- **The post you choose.** When you click "Draft comment" on a post, the text
  of that post (and, for some posts, the image) is sent to our secure server so
  it can write a suggested comment. We only do this for the post you pick.
- **Your email (optional).** If you give your email, we use it to sign you in
  and to send you an optional summary of your activity. Nothing else.
- **Your settings.** Your tone and topic choices are saved on your own computer
  so you do not have to set them again.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not post, like, or comment on your behalf. You always click Post.
- We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
- We do not use your data for advertising, credit, or lending.
- We do not read other tabs or pages. The extension only works on LinkedIn and
  Instagram, and only when you click our button.

## Where your data goes

The post you choose is sent to our server and to a trusted AI provider only to
create the suggested comment. We do not share it with anyone else.

## Keeping data

We keep a record of comments you drafted so the tool can avoid repeating itself
and show you your own activity. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

## Not affiliated

Engagement Assistant is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to
LinkedIn or Instagram.
